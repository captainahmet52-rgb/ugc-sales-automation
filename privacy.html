Privacy Policy

UGC Sales Automation · Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who we are

UGC Sales Automation is a private, single-user tool operated by Ahmet Kadir Atlı (Ayazlı Mahallesi, Furkan Sokak No: 2 Daire: 4, 52400 Fatsa / Ordu, Türkiye; ahmetkadiratli4@gmail.com). The only Google account connected to it is the operator's own account.

Google user data we access

  • Sending email: the tool sends business emails, follow-ups and replies from the operator's Gmail account.
  • Reading email: the tool searches the operator's inbox only for replies to its own business emails (by their subject line) and for delivery-failure notices. Other messages are not read. Replies that do not belong to one of its own email threads are ignored and not stored.

What we store

For the business contacts it emails, the tool stores: company name, company website, the publicly listed business email address and the page where it was found, Gmail message and thread identifiers, the dates of sent emails, a short category for each reply (for example "price question" or "unsubscribe"), and an unsubscribe list. It does not store the full text of emails. Data is kept on the operator's private server and backed up there for up to 14 days.

How we use and share it

The data is used only to run this outreach tool: to avoid contacting the same company twice, to answer replies, to stop follow-ups when someone replies, and to honour unsubscribe requests permanently. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Short notifications about replies that need a human decision are sent to the operator's own Telegram account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

UGC Sales Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Unsubscribing and deletion

Anyone who receives an email from this tool can reply "no thanks" or "unsubscribe" and will not be contacted again. To have your business contact data deleted, email ahmetkadiratli4@gmail.com. The operator can revoke the tool's Gmail access at any time from their Google Account settings.
