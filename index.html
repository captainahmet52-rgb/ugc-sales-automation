UGC Sales Automation

A private tool operated by Ahmet Kadir Atlı.

UGC Sales Automation is a single-user tool that helps one independent creator offer short AI-generated, UGC-style product videos to clothing brands. It sends a small number of business emails from the operator's own Gmail account, reads the replies to those emails, answers simple questions (such as price or delivery time) with fixed, pre-approved text, and notifies the operator about everything else.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
